Leaderboard

Developed in 2015, the Longevity Leaderboard tracks individuals across the world, showcasing who is living the most optimal lifestyles.

The Trueprint Longevity Score

The Trueprint Longevity Score is calculated based on an indvidual's biomarkers using a proprietary algorithm. Data may be connected automatically for those who own the Freedom Bot or are incubated in a Vita-Pod. Those who do not must submit 3,325 biomarker readings in order to be considered for the leaderboard.
